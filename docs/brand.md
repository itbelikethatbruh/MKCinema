# MKCinema brand guide

How MKCinema talks about itself and how it looks, everywhere: the app, the website, the README, release notes and store listings. When copy and this guide disagree, change the copy.

## Who we are

**MKCinema is tomorrow's cinema, for the whole house.**

It is a free home cinema app for the whole family. It brings everything you watch into one place, plays it with a player made for films, gives everyone in the house their own seat, and connects every screen you own, from the TV in the living room to the phone in your pocket.

You bring the sources: Stremio-compatible addons, your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, live TV playlists, or the streaming services you already pay for. MKCinema makes the evening around them.

### Taglines

| Use | Line |
|---|---|
| Primary (hero, store, social) | **Tomorrow's cinema. For the whole house.** |
| Short (badges, page titles) | **The cinema for the whole house** |
| One sentence | A free home cinema app for the whole family, on every screen. |
| Promise | Free forever. No ads, no paywalls, no account needed. |

### What we are not

Say what MKCinema is, never what it isn't compared to someone else. Don't describe it as "a player for addons", "a client" or "a wrapper": addon support is one part of the product, not its identity. Don't name other apps, and don't claim "first" or "only". When a feature really is rare, say "we haven't seen it anywhere else".

## The five pillars

Every page, tour and announcement draws from these, in this order.

1. **Watch.** A player made for films: Smart Play picks the best source, failover keeps it playing, skip intro and credits, X-Ray, styled subtitles, sound modes and HDR on TVs.
2. **Discover.** A Home that's yours: Your Picks worked out on the device, Universes, Peak Cinema, Anime, Live TV, sports and Split Screen.
3. **Together.** Watch parties with voice and cameras, on any version of the film. Couch match, film night, Listen on phone and Share this moment.
4. **The whole family.** A seat for everyone, kids seats by age rating, shows per day, Family Helper, sensory-friendly mode, and every seat its own language.
5. **Every screen.** Web, Android, Android TV and Fire TV, Windows, Mac, Linux, LG and Samsung TVs, with the phone as remote, assistant (Miko), second screen and camera.

Underneath all five: **free forever, private by default, open source.**

## Voice

- **Warm and plain.** Write like a friend who runs a small, very good cinema. Short sentences, everyday words.
- **Concrete.** Say what happens: "the next source takes over by itself", not "seamless resilience".
- **Calm confidence.** No hype words (revolutionary, ultimate, insane), no exclamation marks, no emoji in product copy.
- **Families, not power users.** Lead with the evening (who's watching, on which screen), then the technology.
- **Futuristic in feel, not in jargon.** The future is the house that just works: the phone becomes the remote, the subtitles go to your glasses, grandparents get help from across town.
- **Neutral about sources.** Debrid copy stays neutral: no service is recommended or sounds sponsored, and no source addon is named or recommended.
- **Credit.** MKCinema is made by Dee Lavish. No other name.

## Look

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Default theme | Noir: matte black `#050505`, platinum type `#f3f0e9`, gold accent `#d8c29a` (deep gold `#a8946e`) |
| Headings | Fraunces, optical size 72; italics in gold for the second half of a headline |
| Body | Onest, 400 to 600 |
| Labels | Onest 600, uppercase, letter-spaced (0.2 to 0.26 em), gold, often with a short rule before them |
| Lines | Fine hairlines (`rgba(255,255,255,.08)`), 16 to 24 px corners |
| Futuristic layer | Blueprint grid, corner brackets, slow rings and a soft scan line. Motion uses transform and opacity only and stops for reduced motion |
| Logo | The round MK monogram (`docs/readme/logo.png`, `site/icon-512.png`). Don't recolour, stretch or animate it |

Other themes (Midnight, Velvet, Sepia, Anime) are choices for viewers inside the app. Marketing uses Noir.

## Pictures

- Real screenshots of the live app only. No mockups, renders of fake screens or invented data.
- Film footage in our shots comes from Blender open movies (CC BY); faces in party shots are stock photos. Say so in a caption.
- Other companies' logos appear only in a "works with" row, with a line saying MKCinema isn't affiliated with them.
- Never show a credit card, card imagery, or bank wording.
