MKCINEMA

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026. This page covers the MKCinema web app at mkcinema.pages.dev and the MKCinema apps for Android, Android TV, Fire TV, LG webOS, Windows, Mac and Linux, which all load that web app.

The short version. MKCinema works without an account. If you make one, your library and settings sync through it. We don't sell data, show ads or track you across sites. You can delete your account and everything synced with it in the app at any time.

What stays on your device

Your watchlist, watch progress, watched history, installed addons, settings, profiles and any keys you enter are saved in the app's storage on your device. Your recommendations ("Your Picks") are worked out on your device too.

Your account (optional)

  • Your email address and password are used to sign in. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and we never see them.
  • With Cloud sync on, your watchlist, progress, watched history, addon list and settings are copied to your account so other devices can use them.
  • Debrid and service keys are synced only while Settings › Debrid › Sync keys with my account is on. Turn it off to keep them on this device alone.

Logs you choose to send

Nothing is sent unless you press Settings › Help › Send log. A log contains your app version, device and browser type, your settings with all keys and links removed, the names of your addon hosts, and recent error messages. It never contains passwords, debrid or service keys, addon links or stream links. Each log gets a 6-character code, and only the developer can open it. To have a log removed, open a GitHub issue that includes its code.

Visit counts

The web app counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It records the page, the site you came from, your browser, your country and the load speed. It uses no cookies and no identifiers. You can turn it off in Settings › Data › Share anonymous visit counts.

Services your device talks to

MKCinema is a client. To show titles and play them, your device connects directly to the services below. Each one has its own privacy policy.

  • The addons and debrid services you add yourself.
  • Title and artwork sources: Cinemeta and Metahub, Kitsu for anime, and TMDB or Fanart.tv if you add a key.
  • Trailers from Apple (iTunes) and YouTube. Skip-intro times from TheIntroDB. Subtitles from the subtitle addons you install, such as OpenSubtitles.
  • Services you connect yourself: Trakt, Simkl, AniList, MyAnimeList and MDBList.
  • Two small relays run by MKCinema on Supabase. They forward requests for services that block browsers, which are some debrid services and MyAnimeList. Requests pass through them and nothing is stored.

New-episode notifications

In the Android app, the series you follow stay on your phone. The phone checks Cinemeta for new episodes itself, and no server keeps that list.

Deleting your data

  • Account and synced data: go to Settings › Account › Delete account, or open mkcinema.pages.dev/#delete-account and sign in. Your account and everything synced with it are removed at once.
  • This device: uninstall the app, or clear the site data for mkcinema.pages.dev in your browser.
  • Can't sign in? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll explain how to confirm the account is yours.

Children

MKCinema isn't directed at children under 13. Kids seats exist so a parent can limit what a child sees on a shared device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions go to GitHub issues.
