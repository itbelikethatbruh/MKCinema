What MKCinema is

MKCinema is a media player and library. It shows information about titles and plays video from sources that you add: addons, debrid services, your own media servers and your own files. The only video MKCinema adds by itself is official trailers from YouTube and Apple.

MKCinema is free and will stay free. There is no paid plan, nothing is locked behind payment, and donations on Ko-fi are optional and unlock nothing.
