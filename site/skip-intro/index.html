Skip intros, recaps and credits.

MKCinema looks for the start and end of intros, recaps and credits and gives you a Skip button, or skips for you when you turn that on.

Where the times come from

  • Chapters inside the file, when the release has them.
  • Anime skip times for anime that can be matched.
  • A community database of intro, recap and credits times, looked up by show, season and episode.
  • Times your addons supply, when an addon provides them.
  • Your own skips. If you skip the same stretch on earlier episodes, MKCinema remembers it for that series.

How much is covered

We checked popular shows in November 2026. The community database had skip times for about two in three episodes, and a little under half had a known intro. Anime was better covered than other shows. Where no time is known, there is no button, because guessing wrongly is worse than not skipping.

Settings

Look in Settings › Playback for the skip options. Pressing Enter on a keyboard or A on a gamepad presses the Skip button while it shows.

Questions, answered.

Does it skip the waiting clip some debrid sources show?

Short waiting clips are not the film. Recent versions notice a source that sends only a short clip and move on to the next source.

Can I turn it off?

Yes, in Settings › Playback.

Does it work on the TV app?

The Android TV player (TV app 1.9 or newer) shows Skip intro, Skip recap and Next episode using the same times.
