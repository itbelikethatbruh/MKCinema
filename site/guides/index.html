Help and guides

Every guide, in one place.

Short, plain answers for setting up MKCinema and getting the most out of it. Free forever, on every screen.

Set it up

Fire TV and Android TVHow to install MKCinema on a Fire TV Stick, Android TV or Google TV: type one short address into the Downloader app. Free, with remote control support and a phone remote.iPhone and iPadUse MKCinema on iPhone and iPad as a Home Screen web app. Open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Free, with the built-in player and watch parties.Stremio addonsMKCinema is a free, open-source player for Stremio-compatible addons. Paste an addon link, bring your own debrid key, and watch on Android, TV, Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone and the web.

Watch better

Addon health checkPaste a Stremio-compatible addon link and see if it is slow, broken, blocked or has a key problem, with plain fixes. Runs in your browser; your link is never sent to us.MKV in the browserMKCinema plays most MKV files straight in the browser and converts Dolby and DTS audio to a format browsers can play. No plugins, free and open source.Skip intro and creditsMKCinema can skip intros, recaps and credits for you, using chapters in the file, anime skip times and a community database. Free, open source, on every screen.
