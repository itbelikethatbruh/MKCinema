The tour

Everything you want from a cinema.

01 · LIBRARY

Every addon. One library.

Install any Stremio addon by link and watch results arrive from each one the moment it answers. MKCinema ships no catalogue of its own, so what you watch is always your choice.

  • Debrid built in. Bring your own key from whichever service you use. Cached results play straight away.
  • Smart Play. One tap picks the best stream. If a source stalls, the next one takes over by itself.
  • Sync everywhere. Library, progress, addons and settings follow your account. Scrobble to Trakt or Simkl.
02 · PLAYER

A screening room, not a video tag.

Edge-to-edge picture and every option one tab away: Playback, Picture, Audio and More.

  • Volume to 200 % and every audio language the file carries, picked in one tap.
  • Subtitles done properly. Styled anime subtitles, subtitles inside MKV files, OpenSubtitles and a sync strip you drag.
  • One tap to VLC, mpv, Infuse, MX Player, IINA or PotPlayer. Only players your device supports are listed.
03 · LIVE

Four screens. One sofa.

Split Screen plays up to four channels or streams at once, two on TVs and phones. Select a tile and the sound follows it.

  • Live TV & Guide. Your own M3U playlist or Xtream login with an XMLTV guide, plus channels from your addons.
  • Sports. Live sports from your own sources, sorted by sport.
  • From anywhere. Open Split Screen from Live TV or straight from any film page.
04 · TOGETHER

Same scene, different sofas.

Start a party, share a six-letter code, and everyone watches from their own sources. Drift is corrected quietly, so nobody hears the punchline first.

  • Play, pause and seek in sync with chat, reactions and push-to-talk.
  • Listen on phone. The film plays on the TV, the sound comes out of your phone. Headphones in, house asleep.
  • Your phone as a remote. Scan the code on the TV for a touchpad, D-pad and keyboard. No app to install.
05 · LOOKS

Five looks. Any colour. An Anime mode.

Noir, Midnight, Velvet, Sepia and Anime, plus a colour wheel that tints the whole app. Every look stays dark so the poster art leads, and your choice follows your account.

  • Anime mode. Trending and airing rows, Skip Opening from any addon, and AniList and MyAnimeList progress.
  • Custom posters from a poster service you choose, plus plugins and themes from a reviewed, signed gallery.
06 · BIG SCREEN

Made for the sofa.

Browse with your remote's arrows and OK, and play, pause and skip from its media keys. Android TV, Fire TV, LG and Samsung all get the same cinema.

  • Ambient Mode. When you step away, Home becomes slowly moving film art. OK opens the film.
  • Skip Intro and next episode with a countdown while the next sources warm up.

How to install on Android
  1. Tap Download APK on your Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  4. Open MKCinema from your home screen. Sign in and your addons, library and settings come with you.

MKCinema isn't on Google Play, so Android warns about apps installed from outside the store. Only download the APK from this page.

Automatic updates without Google: install Obtainium (free and open source), then tap Get updates with Obtainium on your phone, or add github.com/itbelikethatbruh/MKCinema in Obtainium by hand. It installs each new MKCinema straight from our GitHub releases. If it asks which file, pick MKCinema.apk.

How to install on a TV
  • Fire TV: install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore. In Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options, allow Downloader to install unknown apps (if Developer options is missing, open My Fire TV › About and press Select on your device name 7 times). Open Downloader, enter tv.mkcinema.app and tap Install.
  • Android TV and Google TV: install Downloader from Google Play, allow it under Settings › Apps › Security › Unknown sources, then enter tv.mkcinema.app. You can also copy MKCinema-TV.apk over with a USB stick and open it with a file manager.
  • LG webOS (beta): LG only installs apps from its own store, so the .ipk needs the TV in Developer Mode (LG's Developer Mode app and the webOS CLI) or Homebrew Channel.
  • Samsung (2023 or newer, beta): MKCinema runs through TizenBrew, a free community app loader. On the TV open Apps, press 1 2 3 4 5, turn Developer mode on with your computer's IP address and restart the TV. On the computer, run TizenBrew Installer. Then in TizenBrew open Module manager › Add GitHub module and type itbelikethatbruh/mkcinema@main/tizen.

On any TV, VLC works best for playing streams.

How to install on Windows
  1. Click Download .exe and open MKCinema.exe. There is no installer; it runs straight away.
  2. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Move MKCinema.exe anywhere you like and pin it to the taskbar or Start.

MKCinema uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which comes with Windows 10 and 11. If it's missing, MKCinema offers Microsoft's download page.

How to install on Mac
  1. Open MKCinema-1.1.1-mac.zip (Safari unzips it into Downloads) and drag MKCinema into Applications.
  2. Open MKCinema from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app for malware; click Done (or OK).
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to MKCinema, then confirm.

This happens because the Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. You only need to do it once.

How to install on Linux
  • Ubuntu, Debian, Mint, Pop!_OS: download the .deb and run sudo apt install ./mkcinema_1.1_amd64.deb, or open it with your software centre.
  • Fedora, Arch and others: install WebKitGTK 4.1 (sudo dnf install webkit2gtk4.1 or sudo pacman -S webkit2gtk-4.1), unpack the .tar.gz and run ./install.sh.

Find MKCinema in your app menu. Ctrl+Q quits, F5 reloads.

Check a download

Every file here is listed with its SHA-256 checksum in SHA256SUMS, and each release on GitHub carries the same list. Compare the number you get with the one in the list before you install.

  • Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash .\MKCinema.exe -Algorithm SHA256
  • Mac: shasum -a 256 MKCinema-1.1.1-mac.zip
  • Linux: put the file next to SHA256SUMS and run sha256sum --ignore-missing -c SHA256SUMS
  • Android: on a computer, sha256sum MKCinema.apk

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is MKCinema?
A premium player for Stremio-compatible addons. It brings your catalogues, sources and subtitles together in one library you can style your way, with a player built for phones, tablets and big screens.
Does MKCinema provide movies or shows?
No. MKCinema hosts no titles, channels or streams. Catalogues come from Cinemeta, subtitles from OpenSubtitles, and the only built-in source is official trailers. Anything else comes from addons you choose to install, and you are responsible for using them lawfully where you live.
Is it really free?
Yes, and it stays free forever: MKCinema will never charge for anything. Every feature is included, with no ads and no paid tier, and the code is public on GitHub under GPL-3.0. You can support development on Ko-fi if you like. Donations never unlock anything.
Do I need an account?
No. Everything works without one. Signing in with email and password adds sync of your library, progress, addons and settings across your devices.
What is debrid, and which service should I use?
A debrid service fetches files for you and streams them back over a fast, direct connection. MKCinema works with the major debrid services using your own key, so use whichever you prefer. Settings › Get started walks you through adding it.
Why do you recommend VLC?
Browsers can't decode some common formats, such as HEVC video or Dolby and DTS sound. VLC plays nearly everything, and MKCinema hands a stream to it in one tap. If you can't install a player, turn on Browser only mode.
Where are my keys stored?
In your browser, and, if you sign in, in your own account row, which row-level security keeps readable only by you. Turn off Sync keys with my account in Settings › Debrid to keep them on one device. Keys are only ever sent to the service they belong to. When a browser isn't allowed to call that service directly, the call passes through MKCinema's small relay, which forwards it and doesn't store or log your key.
Can I move over from Stremio?
Yes. Settings › Account › Import from Stremio copies your library, progress and addons. Your Stremio password is used once and never stored.
Can we see each other in a watch party?
Yes. In the party panel, tap Camera to show your face to everyone in the party. On a TV, use your phone as the camera: open the phone remote, tap Party camera, then Camera. Faces sit in a slim column during the film, get big in the lobby and once the credits roll, and the person talking gets a ring. Video goes straight between your devices and is never recorded. It needs MKCinema on an https address, such as app.mkcinema.app.
What does Sensory-friendly mode do?
Like a sensory-friendly cinema showing, it softens sudden loud bangs and dims flashing light as it happens, all on your device. Turn it on in the player under Picture, or in Settings › Playback. Dimming needs a source that lets the app read the picture, and the first flash can still get through, so it is not a medical safeguard.
Can I read the subtitles in smart glasses?
Yes. Brilliant Labs Frame and Even Realities G1 and G2 connect over Bluetooth: on a computer with Chrome or Edge, open the player, then More › Glasses; on a phone, open the phone remote, tap Subtitles, then the glasses button. Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses get a link to add in the Meta AI app. TVs and the desktop apps can’t reach Bluetooth glasses themselves, so use the phone remote there.
What can Miko, the remote’s assistant, do?
Miko lives in the phone remote. Type or say what you want: pause, back 30 seconds, go to 1:20:00, subtitles off, who’s in this, play the next episode. Everyday asks are understood on the phone itself, free and with no account. For open conversation you can add your own AI key, which stays on your phone and goes only to that service.
Are the downloads safe?
Every app is MKCinema itself, and its full source is on GitHub. The APK is signed with MKCinema's own key. It asks for: internet and network state (to stream and sync); microphone and audio settings (push-to-talk in watch parties and voice search); keep awake and a foreground service (so downloads finish with the screen off); install apps (only to install MKCinema's own updates, after you tap Update); notifications and start at boot (new-episode alerts, checked twice a day); and, on Android TV, the TV home screen (to show your Continue watching row). Android asks you first before the microphone is used, and on Android 13 or later before notifications are shown. The Windows and Mac apps aren't code-signed by Microsoft or Apple yet, so each asks you to confirm the first time. Download them only from this site or the GitHub repository.
