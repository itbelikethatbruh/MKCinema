Layout Studio

Build your Home, then try it.

Pick a look, a card size and a banner. The preview shows it on a computer, a TV and a phone. Press Try it in MKCinema and it opens on your own Home, with your own films, and Undo is one press away.

Try it in MKCinema opens the layout in the app on this device, and nothing changes until you press Use this layout. To put it on a TV or Fire TV Stick, press Send layout to TV there. The TV shows a code under Settings › Home › Layout Studio › Get from phone or computer. Signed in to the same account on both? Save is enough: the TV follows by itself.
Try it in MKCinema

How it works

  • Your own Home. In the app, Layout Studio draws the preview from your real rows and posters. Here it uses popular films as stand-ins.
  • Rows too. In the app you can also move and hide every row, addon catalogs included, and each seat keeps its own.
  • Every screen. The same floating menu on TVs, phones and computers, and every choice works with a TV remote.
  • Synced. Save it once and it reaches your other devices through your MKCinema account.
  • No typing on the TV. Build it on your computer or phone, press Send layout to TV and type the short code the TV shows. The TV only has to show the code.
  • Private. The layout travels inside the link. Your addons, keys and library are never in it, and nothing is stored on a server.
Search, Settings and the menu are always there, whatever the layout. In the app, open Settings › Home › Layout Studio.
