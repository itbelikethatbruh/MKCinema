Works with your addons

A free player for Stremio addons, on every screen.

MKCinema speaks the Stremio addon protocol, so the addons you already use for catalogues, subtitles and streams work here. You get a player made for films, a seat for everyone in the house, and apps for phones, TVs and computers. It is free forever and open source.

Add an addon in three steps

  1. Open the Addons page in MKCinema.
  2. Paste the addon's link (it ends in manifest.json). MKCinema reads it and lists what it offers.
  3. Open any film or show. Sources from your addons appear in the source list, and Smart Play picks the best one that works on your device.

Each person brings their own addons and debrid key. MKCinema hosts no titles, channels or streams. The only built-in source is official trailers.

Moving over from Stremio

Go to Settings › Account › Import from Stremio. Your library, progress and addons are copied over. Your Stremio password is used once to fetch them and is never stored.

What you get on top of the addons you have

  • Source failover. If a source does not start in time, errors, or stalls, the next best one takes over at the same spot.
  • A real film player. Keyboard and remote friendly, subtitle styling and sync, audio track menu, and styled anime subtitles.
  • Watch parties with voice and cameras, and a phone that works as a remote.
  • Skip intro, recap and credits where times are known. How skipping works.
  • Your keys stay yours. Debrid keys sync only to your own account row, and you can switch that off in Settings › Debrid.

Every screen

Android, Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV, Windows, Mac, Linux, LG webOS (beta), Samsung TV (beta), and any browser. On iPhone and iPad, open the web app and add it to your Home Screen.

Questions, answered.

Do I need a Stremio account?

No. You can add addons by pasting their link. An account is only needed if you want to import your Stremio library.

Does MKCinema include movies or shows?

No. It hosts nothing. Catalogues, subtitles and streams come from addons you choose to install, and you are responsible for what you watch.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no paid tier, and the code is public on GitHub under GPL-3.0.

Which debrid services work?

The major debrid services work with your own key. Use whichever you prefer.
