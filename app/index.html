Live Cinemeta is unreachable from here.

MKCinema has no catalog of its own, so nothing can show until Cinemeta answers. This usually means the page is inside a sandbox that blocks outside requests (like the Artifact preview) or you are offline. Open the downloaded index.html in a normal browser, or run mkcinema-server.js and visit it.
