MKCINEMA

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026. This page covers the MKCinema web app at app.mkcinema.app (also reachable at mkcinema.pages.dev) and the MKCinema apps for Android, Android TV, Fire TV, LG webOS, Samsung, Windows, Mac and Linux, which all load that web app.

The short version. MKCinema works without an account. If you make one, your library and settings sync through it. We don't sell data, show ads or track you across sites. You can delete your account and everything synced with it in the app at any time.

What stays on your device

Your watchlist, watch progress, watched history, installed addons, settings, profiles and any keys you enter are saved in the app's storage on your device. Your recommendations ("Your Picks") are worked out on your device too.

Your account (optional)

  • Your email address and password are used to sign in. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and we never see them.
  • With Cloud sync on, your watchlist, progress, watched history, addon list and settings are copied to your account so other devices can use them.
  • Debrid and service keys are synced only while Settings › Debrid › Sync keys with my account is on. Turn it off to keep them on this device alone.

Logs you choose to send

Nothing is sent unless you press Settings › Help › Send log. A log contains your app version, device and browser type, your settings with all keys and links removed, the names of your addon hosts, and recent error messages. It never contains passwords, debrid or service keys, addon links or stream links. Each log gets a 6-character code, and only the developer can open it. Logs are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have one removed sooner, email legal@mkcinema.app with its code.

Watch parties, phone remote and voice chat

When you start or join a watch party, pair a phone as a remote, or use voice chat, the devices find each other through Supabase Realtime, which passes short messages along (play, pause, position, chat) and doesn't keep them. Where the network allows, the devices then talk to each other directly. To set up that direct link they ask public STUN servers run by Google and Cloudflare for their own internet address. As with any video call, the other people in the party can see your IP address. Voice is sent live between devices and never recorded or stored.

Visit counts

The web app counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It records the page, the site you came from, your browser, your country and the load speed. It uses no cookies and no identifiers. You can turn it off in Settings › Data › Share anonymous visit counts.

Services your device talks to

MKCinema is a client. To show titles and play them, your device connects directly to the services below. Each one has its own privacy policy.

  • The addons and debrid services you add yourself.
  • Title and artwork sources: Cinemeta and Metahub, Kitsu for anime, and TMDB or Fanart.tv if you add a key. MKCinema uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Trailers from Apple (iTunes) and YouTube. Skip-intro times from TheIntroDB. Subtitles from the subtitle addons you install, such as OpenSubtitles.
  • Services you connect yourself: Trakt, Simkl, AniList, MyAnimeList and MDBList, your own Plex or Jellyfin server, and a custom poster service such as Pictorium if you add its link.
  • Extra details when you use them: OMDb and RPDB scores if you add a key, Wikipedia and Wikidata for X-Ray, AniSkip, Jikan and arm.haglund.dev for anime episode matching, and Cloudflare's speed test server for Test my connection.
  • Fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg. Like any website, these servers see your IP address when they send the files.
  • Captions made by AI. When you ask MKCinema to make captions, the speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and kept in your browser. The film’s sound is read from your own source and turned into captions on your device; it is never sent to MKCinema or anyone else.
  • Subtitle translation. When no subtitle exists in your language and your browser has no built-in translator, the subtitle lines (and nothing else) are sent to MKCinema’s translation service at subs.mkcinema.app, which runs on Cloudflare Workers AI. Translations are cached by their text so the same file is not translated twice; nothing about you is stored. Turn it off in Settings › Subtitles.
  • Two small relays run by MKCinema on Supabase. They forward requests for services that block browsers, which are some debrid services and MyAnimeList. Requests pass through them and nothing is stored.

Extras in the computer and Android apps

  • Torrent engine (Windows and Android apps, off by default). If you turn it on, your device joins BitTorrent swarms to fetch the file, and other people in each swarm can see your IP address.
  • Discord status (Windows app, off by default). If you turn it on, the title you're watching is sent to the Discord app on your computer, which shows it on your profile.

New-episode notifications

In the Android app, the series you follow stay on your phone. The phone checks Cinemeta for new episodes itself, and no server keeps that list.

Deleting your data

  • Account and synced data: go to Settings › Account › Delete account, or open app.mkcinema.app/#delete-account and sign in. Your account and everything synced with it are removed at once.
  • This device: uninstall the app, or clear the site data for app.mkcinema.app (and mkcinema.pages.dev, if you used it) in your browser.
  • Can't sign in? Email legal@mkcinema.app from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you.

Your choices

  • See or move your data: Settings › Data › Setup Cloner exports your library, addons and settings as one file or code.
  • Correct it: change anything in the app; with Cloud sync on, your account updates within seconds.
  • Stop syncing: turn off Cloud sync or key syncing, or sign out. Nothing new is uploaded after that.
  • Delete it: see "Deleting your data" above.

Children

MKCinema isn't directed at children under 13. Kids seats exist so a parent can limit what a child sees on a shared device.

Changes

The rules for using MKCinema are in the terms of use. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Privacy questions and requests go to legal@mkcinema.app.
