<br>

> **MKCinema** is a free, open-source premium player for the Stremio addons you choose. It brings your catalogues, debrid account and subtitles together in one library you can style your way (five looks, a colour wheel and an Anime mode), with a player built for phones, tablets, desktops and TVs that can even send the sound to your phone.
>
> It ships no titles, channels or streams of its own. Catalogues come from Cinemeta, subtitles from OpenSubtitles, and the only built-in source is official trailers. Everything else comes from addons you install yourself.

> [!NOTE]
> The Windows and Mac apps are not code-signed by Microsoft or Apple yet. On Windows, click **More info › Run anyway** the first time. ## Free, all of it

Everything below ships today and costs nothing. No plans, no ads.

- **Seats** for the whole house (main seat plus 7), each with its own watchlist, progress, picks and Home, and an optional PIN.
- **Kids seats by age rating** where you live (US, Brazil, India, UK or Germany), not just by genre. Pick up to 6, 9 or 12.
- **Arrange Home:** hide, move or rename any row, addon catalogs included. Each seat keeps its own, synced with your account.
- **Source rules** across every addon: lowest resolution, largest or smallest file, preferred audio language, blocked words.
- **Subtitles that sync themselves** in the built-in player, plus a delay you can drag into place.
- **Skip intro**, ending and credits.
- **Downloads** in the Android and Windows apps.
- **Four looks** and a colour wheel.

Coming from Stremio? **Settings › Account › Import from Stremio** (also in the welcome tour) copies your addons, library, Continue watching and watched episodes in one step. Your password goes only to Stremio and is never saved.

<summary><b>Notes and sources</b></summary>

1. Plex removed Watch Together from its new mobile and TV apps in 2025. It stays in the Plex web app, without voice. ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/plex-watch-together-removed-3529939/))

4. Needs a Plex Pass.
5. Jellyfin 10.10 can show a skip button, but a plugin has to find the intros first. Support varies by client.
6. These apps offer a subtitle delay setting only.
7. Up to 6 profiles, PINs and child-safe profiles come with Stremio's paid Supporter plan ($39.99 a year, since June 2026). The free plan has one profile per account. ([Stremio plans](https://www.stremio.com/plans))
8. You need the app installed on the phone (Plex, Jellyfin, or Kore/Yatse for Kodi).
9. Plex Live TV needs a tuner. M3U playlists need a third-party bridge.
10. Plex and Jellyfin need a media server that you run yourself to play your own files.
11. The Stremio apps are open source, but its streaming server is not.
12. Stremio has no plugin or theme support of its own. Third-party mods add some to its desktop app.
13. Some Stremio addons and Kodi add-ons have their own filters, but each is set up separately inside that addon. Neither app filters across all sources.
14. Some TV makers offer private listening through their own remote app (Roku, for example), but only for their own devices. None of these four apps has it built in.
15. Nuvio connects Jellyfin and Emby (both marked beta on [nuvio.tv](https://nuvio.tv)). We found no Plex support.
16. Nuvio's watch party has synced playback, QR invites and text chat. It has no voice: the only microphone use in its code is voice search.
17. Nuvio's filters (resolution, file size, audio, hide or only) apply to its built-in Direct Debrid results, not to every addon.
18. Nuvio has automatic subtitle sync and a delay setting, but no drag-to-sync or transcript.
19. Nuvio's QR codes open phone pages for sign-in, addons and settings. There is no playback remote.
20. Nuvio has plugins, added by repository URL, and custom theme colours. There is no reviewed gallery.
21. "NuvioWeb" is Nuvio's build for Samsung and LG TVs. There is no public browser version.
22. Nuvio has up to six profiles with PINs, but no kids mode based on age ratings.
23. When playback fails, Nuvio can retry in its other player engine (ExoPlayer or mpv). It doesn't move to another source by itself.

</details>

<sub>Checked November 2026 against each app's public docs and issue trackers, and for Nuvio its [site](https://nuvio.tv), [FAQ](https://nuvio.tv/faq) and [source on GitHub](https://github.com/NuvioMedia). Apps change, so open an issue if something's out of date.</sub>

### Coming from VLC?

VLC is a great file player, and MKCinema keeps it one tap away for the formats a browser can't decode. What MKCinema adds is everything around the player:

- **A library, not a file list.** Posters, ratings, your watchlist, Continue watching and new episodes, synced across every device you sign in on.
- **Sources found for you.** Your addons and debrid service are searched at once, fakes and cam copies are filtered out, and Smart Play picks the best one. If it stalls, the next one takes over.
- **Things a file player can't do.** Listen on your phone, watch parties with voice, Skip Intro on any episode, X-Ray, and your phone as a remote by scanning a code.
- **Your settings follow the film.** Speed per title and subtitle timing per file are remembered, and you pick up at the same second on another device.

<p align="center"><img src="docs/readme/how.svg" width="560" alt="MKCinema runs on your device, with Your Picks on-device. It connects to the Stremio addons you install, your debrid service with your own key, optional Supabase sync to your own row, video players such as VLC, and friends' devices for watch parties, peer to peer."></p>

- **Your keys stay yours.** Debrid keys live in your browser and, if you sign in, in your own Supabase row, which row-level security keeps readable only by you. You can turn sync off and keep them on one device.
- **No tracking.** No ads, no cookies and no profiles. The only measurement is an anonymous visit count (Cloudflare Web Analytics: page, browser, country, load speed), which you can turn off in Settings › Data. It never sees your addons, keys or what you watch, and Your Picks never sends your taste to an outside service.
- **Bring your own sources.** MKCinema only plays what your addons return. You are responsible for using them lawfully where you live.

<a name="build"></a>
<p><img src="docs/readme/h-build.svg" width="100%" alt="Build from source"></p>

<details>
<summary><b>Web app and companion server</b></summary>

```sh
cd app
npm install
node mkcinema-server.js
# App:   http://127.0.0.1:7000/
# Addon: http://127.0.0.1:7000/manifest.json
```

The server adds official trailers as a source, a CORS proxy for streams that need headers, and audio rescue (EAC3, AC3, DTS and TrueHD to AAC) when ffmpeg is installed. The app also runs as static files on any host, such as Cloudflare Pages.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Android</b></summary>

Needs the Android SDK (build-tools 35 and platform 35) and JDK 17 or later. Create your own signing key first:

```sh
cd android
mkdir -p keystore && openssl rand -base64 24 > keystore/password.txt
keytool -genkeypair -keystore keystore/mkcinema.jks -storepass "$(cat keystore/password.txt)" \
  -alias mkcinema -keyalg RSA -keysize 4096 -validity 10000 -dname "CN=MKCinema"
ANDROID_HOME=~/Android/Sdk sh build.sh   # writes build/MKCinema.apk
```

Change `package` in `AndroidManifest.xml` for your own fork so it doesn't clash with official releases.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>LG webOS</b></summary>

```sh
npx @webos-tools/cli ares-package webos -o build   # writes build/com.mkcinema.app_1.1.0_all.ipk
```

Install it on a TV in Developer Mode with `ares-install`. See [`webos/README.md`](webos/README.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Windows</b></summary>

Builds from Windows, Linux or macOS with Go 1.24 or later. See [`windows/BUILD.md`](windows/BUILD.md).

```sh
cd windows
go install github.com/akavel/rsrc@latest
rsrc -arch amd64 -ico mkcinema.ico -manifest app.manifest -o rsrc_windows_amd64.syso
GOOS=windows GOARCH=amd64 CGO_ENABLED=0 go build -trimpath -ldflags "-s -w -H windowsgui" -o MKCinema.exe .
```
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>macOS</b></summary>

The app is a standard `.app` bundle that runs `main.js` with the system's JavaScript for Automation, so it needs no compiler. To package it on Linux, install `hfsprogs` and build [mozilla/libdmg-hfsplus](https://github.com/mozilla/libdmg-hfsplus), then:

```sh
cd mac
DMGTOOLS=/path/to/libdmg-hfsplus/build sh package.sh   # writes build/MKCinema-1.1-mac.zip (and .dmg)
```
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Linux</b></summary>

```sh
sudo apt install gcc libwebkit2gtk-4.1-dev python3-pil
cd linux
sh package.sh   # writes build/mkcinema_1.0_amd64.deb and the .tar.gz
```
</details>

<a name="faq"></a>
<p><img src="docs/readme/h-faq.svg" width="100%" alt="Questions"></p>

<details>
<summary><b>Does MKCinema provide movies or shows?</b></summary>

No. MKCinema hosts and links to no content. It plays what your own addons return, plus official trailers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which debrid service and player should I use?</b></summary>

Use the debrid service you already have, or compare a few and pick the one that suits you. MKCinema works with all the common ones. For a player, **VLC** is the easy choice: it's free and plays almost any file. Settings › Get started walks you through both.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need an account?</b></summary>

No. Everything works without one. Sign in with email only if you want sync between devices.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is iPhone supported?</b></summary>

No. iPhone and iPad are not supported platforms.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I move from Stremio?</b></summary>

Yes. Settings › Account › Import from Stremio copies your library, progress and addons. Your Stremio password is used once and never stored.
</details>
