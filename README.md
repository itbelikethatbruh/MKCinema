## What your addons get

- **Real ratings.** Ratings addons show IMDb, TMDB and Rotten Tomatoes scores on the film page and an IMDb rating on every episode, not as rows in your source list.
- **No notice clips.** When a debrid addon plays a short notice clip instead of the film, MKCinema says what happened and moves on to the next source.
- **Settings forms.** Addons built with the Stremio addon SDK can be set up right on the Addons screen, with a TV remote too.
- **More for addon developers.** One optional, data-only `mkcinema` field lets an addon pick its row styles, badges, info panels and where its catalogs show, keep a copy of the watchlist, and receive playback events, skip times and device info. No addon code ever runs.

All of this works with the addons you have today. The addon doesn't need to change.

## Free forever

MKCinema will never charge for anything. No paid plan, no ads, nothing locked. [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/mkcinema) donations are optional and unlock nothing.

MKCinema ships no titles or streams of its own. It plays what your addons return, plus official trailers.
