MKCinema

Get MKCinema on your TV

On a Fire Stick or Android TV, open the Downloader app and type mkctv.pages.dev, then press Go. The app downloads and asks to install.

Download the TV app
  1. First time only: install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore (search "Downloader").
  2. Allow it to install apps: Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps › Downloader › On. If Developer options is missing, open My Fire TV › About and press Select on your device name 7 times.
  3. In Downloader, type mkctv.pages.dev and press Go, then choose Install.
  4. Open MKCinema, then sign in by scanning the QR code with your phone (Settings › Account).

Works on Fire TV, Android TV and Google TV. Phones and computers: mkcinema-site.pages.dev
